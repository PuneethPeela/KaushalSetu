What makes this different

Most workforce dashboards report what's already short-staffed. This system is built around one different question: what will be short-staffed next, and what do we do about it today?
Predicts, doesn't just report
Combines skill-level growth, district sector growth, employer-reported gaps, and emerging-tech signals into one forward-looking score at 6/12/24-month horizons — instead of only counting today's job postings.
Ends in an action, not a chart
Every flagged shortage converts into a concrete number: additional seats, trainers, and labs, plus whether the skill needs a brand-new curriculum module or just more capacity in an existing course.
Explainable by design
Every prediction traces back to the real signals that produced it — growth rate, sector growth, skills gap, obsolescence risk — visible in the "Why this prediction" panel. No black-box score.
Curriculum checked against reality
Alignment is measured against skills that actually co-occur with a course's content in real job postings — not against someone's guess at an "ideal" syllabus.

HOW THE SEAT NUMBER WAS CALCULATED
Estimated demand (headcount) is derived from real posting volume scaled by the predicted 12-month growth multiplier, compared against actual available seats (total_capacity − current_enrollment) at training centres offering a mapped course for this skill. Trainers and labs use a placeholder ratio (60 seats/trainer, 300 seats/lab) — tune this in config.py once real ratios are available.
